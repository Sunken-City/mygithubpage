Dragon Drop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Who we are

Dragon Drop is a game developed and published by Cloudy Games LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers Dragon Drop on every platform where we distribute it, including Google Play, the App Store and Steam.

Questions about this policy or your data: business@cloudyga.me.

Summary

  • Dragon Drop has no accounts, logins or profiles. We never ask for your name, email address or any other personal detail.
  • Your game progress, settings and custom stages are stored on your device. They are not uploaded, and we cannot see them.
  • Data only leaves your device when you play online, or when the game checks which features are switched on. In both cases it is limited to what is technically needed.
  • We do not show ads, sell in-app purchases, run analytics, or sell or share your data.

What is collected, and why

Online multiplayer

Online play is provided by Photon (Exit Games GmbH), a service that relays game traffic between the players in a match. To do that, Photon processes:

  • Your IP address, used to route traffic and choose a nearby server region. Any internet connection needs this.
  • A randomly generated session identifier. On Android and iOS, Dragon Drop connects to Photon anonymously. We send no account or device identifier, and the session identifier is not linked to you across sessions.
  • On Steam only, your Steam ID, so that Steam friends can find each other's matches. Steam is the only platform where a persistent identifier is sent. It goes to Photon, not to us.
  • Gameplay state during a match, such as your character's position, chosen cosmetics and inputs. This is relayed to the other players in your match and is not kept after the match ends.
  • A display name, if the game lets you set one. Other players in your match can see it. You do not have to use your real name, and we recommend that you don't.

We do not receive or store any of this ourselves. Photon's handling of it is governed by Photon's privacy policy.

Feature configuration

Dragon Drop uses ConfigCat to switch features on or off without shipping an update, for example enabling online play. The game downloads a configuration file over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and sends no user identifier, device identifier or advertising identifier. As with any web request, ConfigCat's servers see the IP address the request comes from. See ConfigCat's policies.

App stores

When you download, buy or update the game, the store you use (Google Play, the App Store or Steam) handles that transaction under its own privacy policy. We receive only aggregate sales and installation statistics from the stores. We never see your payment details.

What we do not collect

  • Advertising identifiers. Dragon Drop shows no ads and does not use your advertising ID.
  • Analytics or usage tracking. We do not track how you play, which screens you visit or how long you play for.
  • Location. We do not request your location. Beyond the rough region implied by any IP address, none is collected.
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera. The game never asks for these and cannot access them.
  • Crash or diagnostic reports. Logs stay on your device.
  • Payment information. There are no in-app purchases.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.

Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: to play online and to download the feature configuration.
  • Vibrate: for rumble feedback. You can turn it off in Options.

Photon runs servers in several regions and connects you to a nearby one, which may be outside your country. Where that means a transfer out of the UK or the European Economic Area, Photon's own safeguards apply, as described in its policy.

Security

The feature configuration is downloaded over HTTPS, and Photon encrypts the authentication step of each online connection. Live gameplay traffic during a match is relayed through Photon and is not end-to-end encrypted. It contains no personal information beyond the display name and session identifier described above.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: your progress, settings and custom stages stay until you delete them in the game or uninstall it.
  • At Photon: connection data is kept under Photon's own retention policy. Gameplay state is not stored after a match ends.
  • By us: we hold no personal data, so we have nothing to retain.

Children

Dragon Drop is rated E for Everyone, and we expect children to play it. It collects no personal information from anyone of any age: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Online multiplayer lets players see each other's display names and play together in real time. There is no text or voice chat. Parents deciding whether online play is suitable should know that it matches players with others, including people they don't know.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, and to complain to a data protection authority.

Because we hold no personal data, there is usually nothing for us to act on. To remove everything Dragon Drop has stored on your device, delete your save data in the game's options or uninstall the game. To exercise a right over data held by Photon, see its policy. We are happy to help if you contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Dragon Drop handles data, for example by adding advertising, in-app purchases or analytics, we will update this policy before releasing that change and update the date at the top. We will announce material changes in the game's release notes.
