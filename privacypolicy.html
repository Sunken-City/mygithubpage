Dragon Drop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Who we are

Dragon Drop is a game developed and published by Cloudy Games LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers Dragon Drop on every platform where we distribute it, including Google Play, the App Store and Steam.

Questions about this policy or your data: business@cloudyga.me.

Summary

  • Dragon Drop has no accounts, logins or profiles. We never ask for your name or email address.
  • Your game progress, settings and custom stages are stored on your device. We cannot see them.
  • The mobile versions of Dragon Drop show ads, provided by Unity. Ad providers collect device and usage data to show and measure ads. One in-app purchase removes ads and unlocks the achievement boards.
  • When you first play on mobile, the game asks for your year of birth. That answer stays on your device. It decides how ads behave, and children never get personalised ads.
  • Online play sends only what is needed to connect you to the other players in your match.
  • We do not run our own analytics and we do not sell your data to data brokers.

Your age and how ads work

The first time you play on Android or iOS, Dragon Drop asks for your year of birth. We ask only for the year, and the answer is stored on your device and never sent to us or to anyone else. The game uses it to choose privacy settings for the ad provider:

  • Under 13, or no answer: ads are treated as directed at a child. They are never personalised, your device's advertising ID is not used, and only ad sources certified for Google Play's Families programme are allowed. Purchase offers are not shown.
  • 13 to 15: ads are never personalised.
  • 16 and over: you are asked whether you agree to personalised ads. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not based on your activity in other apps.

You can change your personalised-ads choice at any time in the game's Options.

What is collected, and why

Advertising

Ads in Dragon Drop are served by Unity LevelPlay, which selects ads from Unity Ads (both provided by Unity Technologies). Ads appear between matches, never during play. To show ads, measure them, and prevent ad fraud, Unity's software collects:

  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID (not used for children; see above).
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address. Precise location is never collected.
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped them. Unity collects these within the ad itself, not during gameplay.
  • Diagnostics about how the ad software is performing.
  • Purchase history, such as whether you have bought the ad-free upgrade.

Unity receives this data directly from the game; we do not. Unity's handling of it is governed by the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

If you buy the ad-free upgrade, the game stops loading ads and does not start the ad software at all.

In-app purchase

Dragon Drop offers one optional purchase that removes ads and unlocks the achievement boards. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card or payment details. The purchase is processed in the game by Unity In-App Purchasing, which receives the purchase record and diagnostic information so it can confirm and restore your purchase, for example after a reinstall. Unity does not share this data with third parties.

Online multiplayer

Online play is provided by Photon (Exit Games GmbH), which relays game traffic between the players in a match. Photon processes:

  • Your IP address, to route traffic and pick a nearby server.
  • A random player identifier. On Android and iOS the game creates a random identifier when it is installed and uses it to keep players distinct in a match. It is not your device's advertising ID or any account, and it is replaced if you reinstall the game.
  • On Steam only, your Steam ID, so that Steam friends can find each other's matches.
  • Gameplay state during a match, such as your character's position, chosen cosmetics and inputs. It is relayed to the other players and not kept after the match.
  • A display name, if the game lets you set one. Other players in the match can see it. Please don't use your real name.

See Photon's privacy policy.

Feature configuration

Dragon Drop uses ConfigCat to switch features on or off without an update. The game downloads a configuration file over HTTPS and sends no identifier with it. ConfigCat's servers see the request's IP address. See ConfigCat's policies.

Stored on your device

Your progress, achievements, settings, custom stages, year of birth, ad choices and purchase status are stored locally on your device.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • No precise location.
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera. The game never asks for these.
  • No chat. There is no text or voice chat.
  • No analytics of our own. We do not track how you play. The diagnostics described above are collected by Unity's ad and purchase software.
  • No payment details. Google Play and the App Store handle payment.

Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: online play, ads, purchases and feature configuration.
  • Advertising ID and Android's ad services (attribution and topics): used by the ad software to show and measure ads. Not used for children.
  • Billing: the in-app purchase.
  • Vibrate: rumble feedback. You can turn it off in Options.

Selling and sharing. We do not sell personal data to data brokers. When an adult agrees to personalised ads, the advertising data above is shared with Unity for personalised advertising, which some US state laws (such as California's) treat as "selling" or "sharing". You can opt out at any time by turning off personalised ads in Options. Players under 16 are always opted out.

Security

The ad, purchase and configuration services all use encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Photon encrypts the authentication step of each online connection. Live gameplay traffic during a match is not end-to-end encrypted, but contains no personal information beyond the display name and player identifier described above.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it in the game or uninstall.
  • Unity, Photon and ConfigCat: under each provider's own retention policy. Photon does not keep gameplay state after a match.
  • By us: we hold no personal data.

Children

Dragon Drop is rated E for Everyone, and we expect children to play it. We follow Google Play's Families policy and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). For any player who says they are under 13, or who skips the question:

  • ads are never personalised and the advertising ID is not used;
  • only ad sources certified for Google Play's Families programme are allowed;
  • the game does not show purchase offers.

Online multiplayer lets players see each other's display names and play together in real time. There is no text or voice chat. Parents should know that online play can match a child with people they don't know.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's data has been collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority.

  • Personalised ads: turn them off at any time in the game's Options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device's settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Data held by Unity: Unity's privacy policy explains how to access or delete it. Email us at business@cloudyga.me and we will help you make the request.
  • Data on your device: delete your save data in Options, or uninstall the game.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Dragon Drop handles data, we will update this policy before releasing that change and update the date at the top. We will announce material changes in the game's release notes.
